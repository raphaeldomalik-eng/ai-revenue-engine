# Architecture Boundary

AI Revenue Engine is the portfolio **EXECUTION / RESEARCH** owner.

## Owns

The shared deterministic first-party crawler, provider execution, bounded public-web research, extraction, and selective AI interpretation.

## Must not own

Prestige Nexus canonical identity, source/entity classification authority, product publication, or Event Suite operational state.

Consumers call AIRE through governed Nexus/versioned contracts. Product repositories must not import AIRE implementation directly.

# Event Suite differentiation and claim governance

## Governance rule

Commercial claims are typed with a category (`FEATURE`, `ADVANTAGE`, `SALES_WEDGE`, or `DEFENSIBLE_DIFFERENTIATOR`) and a strength (`DEFENSIBLE`, `ADVANTAGE`, `COMMODITY`, `HYPOTHESIS`, or `PROHIBITED`). A claim may only be used as a defensible differentiator when it is explicitly `DEFENSIBLE`, categorised as a `DEFENSIBLE_DIFFERENTIATOR`, supported by evidence-backed competitor comparison, and has high evidence confidence. Future AI and outreach work must use this gate.

## Known commodities

The following are common market capabilities and must not be positioned as unique by themselves: event page builder, AI content generation, ticketing, QR tickets, RSVP, email marketing, “all-in-one”, easy to use, affordable, and branded event pages.

|---|---|---|
| Event Operations as the centre of gravity | HYPOTHESIS / sales wedge | Customer evidence that connected operational work solves a material problem |
| Growth + Operations | HYPOTHESIS / sales wedge | Evidence that growth and operations together create meaningful value |
| Connected Operations | HYPOTHESIS / sales wedge | Workflow research and competitor comparison |
| Modular entry / land-and-expand | HYPOTHESIS / advantage | Validated entry patterns and expansion evidence |
| Technology + optional human/local delivery | HYPOTHESIS / advantage | Approved delivery model and customer/operator evidence |
| High-frequency event value | HYPOTHESIS / sales wedge | Portfolio evidence; frequency is a future variable with no threshold yet |

“Competitors cannot do this” and equivalent unsupported superiority wording is prohibited. Final competitor evidence and final approved wording are deferred.

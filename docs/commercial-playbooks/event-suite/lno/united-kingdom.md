## Commercial model

An LNO is a channel/business opportunity involving possible opportunity sourcing, customer introduction/onboarding, selected local services/support, and agreed Event Suite module/service delivery. It is not simply a software purchase.

Primary conversion: business opportunity enquiry/application. A software demo is supporting proof, not the default CTA. Human approval remains required for operator opportunities and commercial terms.

## Territory boundary

The proposed UK package rates are £495 / £595 / £795 / £1,095 / £1,495, with corresponding Operator Delivery Prices of £396 / £476 / £636 / £876 / £1,196 and operator-originated commission examples of £99 / £119 / £159 / £219 / £299. The proposed rule is 20% of qualifying net Event Package revenue actually collected, every Portfolio year while the Operator remains originating/servicing, with no automatic decay.

UK Portfolio commitments are proposed for 1–3, 4, 8, and 12+ events over 12 months. The proposed assigned Portfolio support share is 10%, distinct from operator-originated commission. Operator-managed purchases use the Operator Delivery Price and do not earn self-commission. Customer-owned and Operator-managed models are separate.

UK terms are proposed, not final. Do not apply South African rates or rules here; a later UK pricing version may supersede UK-1.0.

## Commercial scope

Event Suite Phase 1 is Event Operations across GROW, MANAGE, and RUN. Venue is a client type, not a separate Phase 1 proposition.

## Entry and conversion

Direct supports self-service signup and qualified live demo as conversion concepts. Routing thresholds are deliberately unresolved, and live demos remain human-led.

## Territory hypothesis

The current working hypothesis is that UK entry may allow operations/growth work alongside an incumbent ticketing platform rather than forcing ticketing migration. This is not an approved rule.

## Client, role, and evidence posture

Initial client and role examples are shared with the Event Suite model and are not ranked. Candidate differentiation remains hypothesis-led; competitor evidence, customer evidence, qualification, and final ICP prioritisation are deferred.

## Proposed pricing

The proposed UK public package prices are £495 Event Essentials, £595 Ticketed Event, £795 Event Growth, £1,095 Event Operations, and £1,495 Complete Event Suite. Proposed Growth Studio public prices are £249 / £449 / £699 for 3 / 6 / 12 pages, with managed growth from £2,495. Proposed Ticketing is 2% core plus 1% servicing capped at £1 per paid ticket; payment processing is separate and genuinely complimentary standard Ticketing has a £0 Event Suite service fee.

Annual portfolio rates, ticketing allocation, large-event review triggers, and configurable commercial items are represented in `src/revenue/pricing.ts`.

`PROPOSED` is not `FINAL` or `CURRENT/APPROVED`. The owner may supersede UK-1.0 with a later version.

## Pricing and human escalation

UK pricing is proposed and versioned; final owner approval and future revisions remain unresolved. Do not copy South African rates or LNO terms. Escalate important commercial conversations, negotiation, and major relationships to a human.

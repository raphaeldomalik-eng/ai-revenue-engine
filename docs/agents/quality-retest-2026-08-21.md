# AI Revenue Engine Agent Pack V1 — Boundary Re-test

**Date:** 2026-08-21
**Result:** **PASS — AGENT PACK V1 IS QUALITY-PROVEN FOR IMPLEMENTATION**

## Method

The four required boundary/stress cases were re-run as structured desk tests
against current public evidence after the three handover corrections were
applied. The purpose was to test the corrected role contracts and deterministic
boundaries, not to generate production prospects or authorise outreach.

Acceptance required:

- correct primary target identity;
- related organisations retained without target contamination;
- supporting and counter-evidence considered for EGS, Ticketing and ECC;
- no unsupported strong opportunity;
- no guessed contact data;
- no contact ownership misattribution;
- no unsafe brand/parent/operator collapse.

## Case 1 — eCommerce Expo

### Identity

Primary target: **CloserStill Media** (`HIGH`).

The official eCommerce Expo site explicitly states that the event is organised
by CloserStill Media, and CloserStill's corporate event library lists eCommerce
Expo in its portfolio. This overrides the historical UPTECH result.

Sources:

- [Official eCommerce Expo site](https://www.ecommerceexpo.co.uk/)
- [CloserStill Media event library — eCommerce Expo](https://www.closerstillmedia.com/events-library/ecommerce-expo)

### Related organisations

- eCommerce Expo — `OPERATING_BRAND`/event brand, not a separate organisation website by default.
- ExCeL London — `VENUE`, not organiser.
- Swapcard — event-app platform/provider, not organiser.
- ASP — website supplier, not organiser.

### Commercial evidence balance

Supporting ECC context includes 10 theatres, 200+ providers/agencies and
co-located event activity. Counter-evidence includes a mature official site and
an app supporting schedules, attendee/exhibitor discovery, connections,
meeting booking, reminders and calendar export.

Source: [eCommerce Expo event app](https://www.ecommerceexpo.co.uk/access-event-app)

Net result:

- EGS: `NO_EVIDENCE`/negative-safe.
- Ticketing: `NO_EVIDENCE`; registration/provider presence is context only.
- ECC: at most `POSSIBLE`, and not commercially advanced without a sourced gap.
- Contact: no guessed data and no supplier contact promoted to CloserStill.

### Related organisations

- London Packaging Week — event brand.
- Easyfairs Group/UK — group and operating context retained without duplicate target creation.

- App/badge technology suppliers — providers, not target contacts.

### Commercial evidence balance

visitor/exhibitor coordination. Official counter-evidence is unusually strong:
the Packaging Plus app provides ticket access, personalised agendas,
AI-powered recommendations, exhibitor discovery, interactive floorplans,
meeting requests, live updates and messaging; smart badges provide NFC/QR lead
capture and post-visit summaries.

Net result:

- EGS: `NO_EVIDENCE`/negative-safe.
- Ticketing: `NO_EVIDENCE`; integrated entry-voucher flow is counter-evidence.
- ECC: `NO_EVIDENCE` or low `POSSIBLE`, not `STRONG_HYPOTHESIS`.
- Contact: Easyfairs/event addresses retain their evidenced ownership class;
  no technology-provider route is promoted to Easyfairs.

## Case 4 — Connected Britain

### Identity

Primary target: **Total Telecom** (`HIGH`) as the operating event/media brand.

The official event story states that Connected Britain is organised by Total
Telecom. Total Telecom's own About page lists Connected Britain in its events
portfolio and states that Terrapinn Holdings Ltd purchased Total Telecom in
2004. Terrapinn is therefore retained as parent/group rather than silently
replacing Total Telecom.

Sources:

- [Connected Britain — Our Story](https://www.terrapinn.com/conference/connected-britain/our-story.stm)
- [Total Telecom — About](https://totaltele.com/about-us/)
- [Connected Britain 2026](https://www.terrapinn.com/conference/connected-britain/index.stm)

### Related organisations

- Terrapinn Holdings Ltd — `PARENT_GROUP` and platform/operator context.
- Connected Britain — event brand.
- ExCeL London — venue.
- Swapcard — app platform/provider.

### Commercial evidence balance

Supporting complexity is substantial: 8,000 attendees, 3,000 organisations,
450 speakers, 300 exhibitors, 14 themes and an awards ceremony. Counter-evidence
includes the Terrapinn Events App/Swapcard web platform with tickets, agenda,
floorplan, exhibitors/sponsors, personalised schedules, attendee messaging and
one-to-one meeting booking, plus a facilitated networking model.

Sources:

- [Connected Britain event app](https://www.terrapinn.com/conference/connected-britain/Event-app.stm)
- [Connected Britain networking](https://www.terrapinn.com/conference/connected-britain/networking.stm)

Net result:

- EGS: `NO_EVIDENCE`; mature brand, event and publishing properties are counter-evidence.
- Ticketing: `NO_EVIDENCE`; ticket/app integration is context/counter-evidence.
- ECC: `POSSIBLE` at most, not `STRONG_HYPOTHESIS` without a sourced gap.
- Contact provenance: `@totaltele.com` routes on the official event pages are
  attributable to Total Telecom functions. An `@terrapinn.com` app-support
  address belongs to the related parent/platform operator and must not be
  relabelled as a Total Telecom buyer email without explicit attribution.
